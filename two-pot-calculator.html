How the two-pot system works

  • Since 1 September 2024, a third of new contributions goes into the savings component and two-thirds into the retirement component.
  • Money saved before then stays in the vested component. A once-off "seed" of 10% of that balance (capped at ) was moved into the savings component on 1 September 2024.
  • You can withdraw from the savings component once per tax year, with a minimum of . The withdrawal is added to your taxable income and taxed at your marginal rate. SARS may also deduct any tax you owe.
  • The retirement component cannot be accessed before retirement. At retirement, it must be used to buy an annuity.
